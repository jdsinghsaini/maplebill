MapleBill – Invoices & Estimates (Android app)
==============================================

HOW TO PUT IT ONLINE (one time, free, about 2 minutes)
1. Unzip MapleBill-app.zip on a computer. You get a folder named "MapleBill".
2. Go to https://app.netlify.com/drop (create a free account with your email).
3. Drag the whole "MapleBill" folder onto the page.
4. Netlify gives you a link like https://your-name.netlify.app
   (You can rename it in Site settings, e.g. northridge-invoices.netlify.app)
   Or use GitHub Pages: put these files in a public repository, then
   Settings → Pages → Deploy from branch → main / (root).

HOW TO INSTALL ON ANY ANDROID PHONE
1. Open the link in Chrome on the phone.
2. Tap "Install" on the banner (or Chrome menu ⋮ → "Install app" / "Add to Home screen").
3. MapleBill appears in the app drawer with its own icon and opens full-screen.
   After the first open it also works with no internet.
Anyone you give the link to can install it the same way. Each phone keeps its own data.

KEEP YOUR DATA SAFE
Everything is stored on the phone. Use Settings → Backup & restore → "Share backup"
to save a copy to Google Drive regularly (e.g. once a month). On a new phone,
open the app and tap "Restore a backup" on the welcome screen.

WANT IT IN THE GOOGLE PLAY STORE?
Go to https://www.pwabuilder.com, paste your link, and choose "Android".
It produces the Play Store package for you (a Google Play developer account costs US$25 once).

UPDATING
Replace the files on your host. Phones pick up the new version the next time
the app is opened online. Data is kept.
